Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026. “Zujaj” (com.smrh.zujaj) is made by SMRH. This page explains what data the app uses and why.

Data that stays on your device

  • Photos: photos you pick for wallpapers or photo widgets are stored inside the app on your device only and are never uploaded. Subject detection runs on the device.
  • Contacts: if you pick a person for a “People” widget, the name and number are kept on your device only.
  • Widget settings and designs: on your device.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your approximate location to calculate prayer times and get the weather. Coordinates are sent to the weather service (MET Norway, api.met.no) to fetch forecasts; we don’t store them on any server of ours. You can pick a city manually instead.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device’s advertising ID and technical information to show and measure ads, under Google’s Privacy Policy. Where the law requires it (e.g. the EU/UK) we ask for your consent first. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device’s Google settings. Subscribers see no ads.

To keep the app free, it checks on your device whether an ad blocker is active (by testing access to ad servers and the “Private DNS” setting). This check is never sent anywhere.

Purchases and codes

Subscriptions are handled by Google Play; we never see your payment details. If you redeem a code, a random app-specific device ID is stored with the code in Firebase (Google) so we know where the code was used and can revoke leaked codes.

Google services

The app uses Firebase (a database for public settings and codes, and App Check for protection), Google Play Billing and ML Kit (on-device). These may collect technical information under Google’s policies.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell your data.
  • No accounts, no email sign-up.
  • We don’t upload your photos or contacts.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.
